# Ukraine Stores — Second Payment Business Case

Bilingual English/Ukrainian business case for charging the final basket amount after picking.

## Decision requested

Prioritize a Ukraine MVP for:

1. authorizing an estimated basket amount;
2. receiving final item quantities, substitutions and packaging after picking;
3. releasing the original hold;
4. charging the final basket amount;
5. preserving item-level transparency and consent.

## Headline evidence

- ₴987k gross Bolt-funded positive settlement differences in Jan–Jun 2026.
- €185.8k / ₴9.45m projected payout in Oct 2026–Mar 2027 under the optimistic Stores forecast.
- 24.2% of forecast commission revenue consumed before shopper, support, payment and other costs.
- ATB, Fora and other large grocery launches materially increase the exposure.

## Method

Existing partners use their historical gross positive payout / Bolt GMV rate. ATB and Fora use the pooled current-grocery benchmark of 2.70%. Other forecast partners use the six-month country benchmark of 1.08%.
